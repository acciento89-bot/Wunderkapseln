## English review note draft

Wunderkapseln is an offline match-3 game with miniature worlds, sequential level progression and earned world abilities. No account or sign-in is required. There is no demo username or password.

Open Play to begin the first available level. Tap two adjacent tiles or swipe to swap them. Invalid swaps do not consume a move. Match targets within the move limit. The world ability becomes available after its meter is charged; select the ability and then a board position. Completing different levels restores world details at eight-level intervals.

Settings contains German/English language selection, sound, reduced motion and haptics options. The Treasures screen includes a one-time local ten-minute alpha gift. Activate it explicitly; only active gameplay consumes its timer. Existing game progress is stored locally and there is no cloud account.

This alpha does not offer functioning in-app purchases or rewarded ads. Planned shop entries are marked unavailable. Do not use these notes for a future monetized binary without revising this paragraph and providing the corresponding sandbox test instructions.
