# Alpha 0.3.0: reliable progress and fair inventory

Continue the approved native iOS/Android miniature-world game without changing the artwork, icon, level recipes, prices or store availability.

## Required behavior

A five-life inventory pack grants five reserve lives regardless of the natural life meter. Natural lives regenerate to five as before and are consumed first. A failed unprotected attempt consumes one reserve life only when the natural meter is empty. Starting or winning a level costs nothing. Protected attempts never use reserve lives. Old saves migrate with zero reserve lives. Reserve capacity is 9,999 and active-time capacity remains 864,000 seconds; a rejected activation never consumes inventory.

Loading is fail-closed: inaccessible, unrecoverably corrupt or newer-format saves cannot silently become new games. A corrupt primary with a readable valid backup may recover. A transient primary-read failure must not restore a stale backup. Retry re-reads storage, restores the original profile and enables play. No writes occur before successful hydration.

Hints should consider visible goals and frost, not future random refills. In the opening three levels the first legal useful swap is highlighted before the first move; on later moves the manual hint remains available. Shared rules drive both clients. A visible-information-only campaign audit reports bot outcomes, never human success rates or proof of solvability.

## Delivery constraints

Preserve the native React Native client, portable preview, DE/EN, offline saves, no forced ads and disabled real payments. Do not retry the previously blocked GitHub upload through another route. Native dependencies are currently unavailable because registry.npmjs.org fails DNS resolution; no native build claim is permitted. Ship source, portable preview and fresh verification evidence.
