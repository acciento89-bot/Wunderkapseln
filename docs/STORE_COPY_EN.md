## Beschreibung (1009 Zeichen)

Tiny worlds. Wonderful moments.

Match colourful tiles and bring miniature worlds under glass to life. From Starlight Garden and Cloud Harbour to Aurora Palace, every capsule holds a new place to explore.

480 levels across 12 worlds
Collect matching tiles, clear frost and reach your goals within the available moves. Create special pieces with larger matches and set off playful chain reactions.

Earn your Wonder Move
Charge your ability through matching. Wind, tide or bloom helps you in a way that fits the world. Choose when to use your earned move.

Watch your world grow
Every eight different level victories reveal a new detail. Bridges, lights and miniature landmarks turn progress into something you can see.

Play at your pace
The campaign works offline. Resume an unfinished level and adjust sound, reduced motion and language in Settings. German and English are included.

No account is required. Your current progress is stored locally on your device. Cross-device cloud backup is not included.
